# Trading & the Economy

Roughly a third of sectors have a **port** that trades three commodities:

- **Fuel Ore** — cheapest, bulkiest
- **Organics** — mid-tier
- **Equipment** — most valuable, and also the raw material for mines

## Buy low, sell high

Every port has a stance on each commodity: it either **sells** it to you
(you buy) or **buys** it from you (you sell). A port never does both for the
same commodity. This is what creates trade routes — buy Equipment cheap at
a port that sells it, haul it to a different sector, and sell it to a port
that buys it, for a profit.

Prices drift with every trade: buying pushes the price up, selling pushes it
down, and stock slowly regenerates over time. Big single trades move the
price more than several smaller ones.

## Stardock (Sector 1)

The Federation shipyard in Sector 1 always **sells** all three commodities
at fair prices and never runs out of stock — a reliable (if not
maximally profitable) place to restock Fuel Ore for the long haul, or
Equipment for building mines.

## Cargo holds

Your ship has a limited number of cargo holds shared between Fuel Ore,
Organics, Equipment, and Colonists. Check your HUD (top bar) for
`holds used / holds total`. Upgrade your holds at the Shipyard if you're
running a serious trade route.

## Tips

- Scout the Galaxy Map for ports before committing turns to a long haul.
- A cosmic storm (see **Random Events**) can spike or crash a port's prices
  overnight — check before you commit to a route you were counting on.
- Corporations can pool knowledge of profitable routes over sector chat.
