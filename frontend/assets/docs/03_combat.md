# Combat & Mines

## Ship-to-ship combat

You can attack any other player or hostile NPC sharing your sector. Combat
resolves in up to five rounds:

1. Each side fires a random portion (10-35%) of its remaining fighters.
2. **Shields absorb damage first.** Once a side's shields are depleted,
   further damage reduces fighters directly.
3. Combat ends early if either side runs out of fighters, or after five
   rounds — whoever has more fighters left is the winner.

Attacking costs **2 turns** and requires at least 1 fighter.

### Outcomes

- **You win:** you loot roughly 20% of a defeated player's credits (or the
  full bounty for an NPC), and the enemy ship is destroyed.
- **You lose:** your ship is destroyed — you eject to an escape pod at
  Stardock with a fresh Merchant Cruiser. You keep your credits minus
  whatever was looted, but lose all cargo.
- **Draw / retreat:** both ships survive, battered, for another round later.

## Hostiles you'll encounter

- **Ferrengi Raiders** — aggressive pirates that spawn randomly across the
  galaxy. Fighting them earns a credit bounty.
- **Space Monsters** — dangerous native creatures with heavier defenses
  but bigger bounties.
- **Derelict / Alien Traders** — non-aggressive wrecks. Salvaging one (i.e.
  attacking it) is safe-ish and rewarding, since it won't fight back with
  much force.

## Mines

You can deploy mines in your current sector, consuming Equipment cargo
1-for-1. Mines are silent, sector-based defenses:

- Any ship (other than the owner or, once corp-shared defenses ship, allies)
  entering a mined sector has a chance to trigger a strike, taking shield and
  fighter damage.
- Bigger mine fields mean a higher trigger chance and more damage.
- Mines are consumed gradually as they detonate.

Mines are useful for guarding a profitable route, a planet you've claimed,
or just making a sector risky for pursuers.
