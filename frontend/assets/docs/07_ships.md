# Ships & the Shipyard

All ship purchases and upgrades happen at **Stardock, Sector 1**.

## Ship classes

| Ship | Price | Fighters | Shields | Holds | Cloak |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Merchant Cruiser | Free (starter) | 500 | 200 | 60 | No |
| Scout Marauder | 15,000 cr | 750 | 150 | 40 | Yes |
| Cargo Hauler | 35,000 cr | 400 | 250 | 120 | No |
| Corporate Flagship | 90,000 cr | 2,000 | 600 | 80 | No |
| Imperial Starship | 175,000 cr | 5,000 | 1,200 | 100 | Yes |

Figures above are **maximums** for that hull — you still have to buy up to
them with upgrades (see below). Each ship starts with a smaller base cargo
hold and a modest 50 fighters / 50 shields when purchased.

Buying a new ship replaces your current one — any cargo aboard your old
ship is left behind at the yard, so sell or deposit it first.

## Upgrades

Fighters, shields, and cargo holds can all be purchased incrementally, up
to your current hull's maximum:

- Fighters: 50 credits/unit
- Shields: 30 credits/unit
- Cargo holds: 2,000 credits/unit

## Choosing a ship

- **Scout Marauder / Imperial Starship** — cloaking lets you slip through
  sectors more discreetly (evasion mechanics are cosmetic in this release;
  full stealth mechanics are on the roadmap).
- **Cargo Hauler** — best holds-per-credit for trade routes.
- **Corporate Flagship / Imperial Starship** — built for combat and
  planet defense.
