# Random Events

The galaxy is alive even when you're not looking. A background events
engine fires roughly every 45-135 seconds, picking one of several ambient
events and announcing it to every connected captain (and logging it for
anyone who checks in later):

- **Ferrengi Raider spawn** — a new pirate ship appears in a random
  sector. Fight it for a credit bounty, or steer clear.
- **Space Monster sighting** — a tougher native hostile appears, with a
  bigger bounty to match the risk.
- **Derelict / Alien Trader discovery** — a mostly-defenseless wreck
  appears, ripe for salvage.
- **Cosmic storm** — a random port's prices spike or crash by 15-40%
  overnight. Great for opportunistic traders watching the feed.
- **Mine field detected** — an unclaimed, neutral mine field appears in a
  random sector. Treat it like any hostile mine field: enter carefully.

## How you find out

- **Live, if you're online:** events stream to your Galaxy Feed panel and
  pop up as a toast notification the moment they happen, over the
  real-time connection.
- **If you're not online:** every event is logged and visible the next
  time you check the feed — nothing is missed, it just arrives whenever you
  reconnect.

Combat you're directly involved in, planet claims, and mine strikes in your
own path also flow through this same feed, alongside the ambient galaxy
events above.
