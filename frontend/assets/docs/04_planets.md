# Planets & Colonization

Roughly 1 in 10 sectors outside Federation space has a planet. Unclaimed
planets are up for grabs — visit one and claim it from the **Planet** tab.

## Planet types

Planets come in five classes (M, K, O, H, C) representing different
environments. This build treats them as cosmetic/flavor for now — every
planet type stores the same resources and supports the same colony
mechanics.

## Managing your colony

Once you own a planet, you can:

- **Deposit** cargo from your ship's holds (Fuel Ore, Organics, Equipment,
  or Colonists) into the planet's storage — useful for stockpiling out of
  harm's way, or seeding a colony with population.
- **Collect** resources back from the planet into your ship's holds when
  you need to move them elsewhere.

Colonists deposited on a planet count toward its population, up to the
planet's maximum capacity. A well-populated, well-defended planet is a
strong foothold — and a target, so consider deploying mines in the sector
to protect it.

## Citadels & defense fighters

Every planet has a citadel level and a defense fighter count. In this
release these are tracked and displayed but not yet upgradeable through the
UI — a foundation for future expansion (see the project README for what's
planned next).
