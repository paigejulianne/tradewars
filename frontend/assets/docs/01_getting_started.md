# Getting Started

Welcome, Captain. **TradeWars: Galactic Frontier** is a persistent, multiplayer
space trading and combat game inspired by the classic BBS door game *Trade
Wars 2002*. You explore a galaxy of hundreds of sectors linked by warp lanes,
trade commodities between ports, build a fleet, colonize planets, fight
pirates and rival captains, and climb the leaderboard.

## Creating an account

1. Register with an email address, password, and a **callsign** (your
   in-game handle).
2. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link. You must
   verify your email before you can log in — this keeps the galaxy free of
   throwaway accounts.
3. Log in. You start at **Sector 1 (Stardock, Federation space)** with:
   - A Merchant Cruiser (20 fighters, 100 shields, 20 cargo holds)
   - 5,000 credits
   - 1,000 turns

## Turns

Most actions — moving between sectors, attacking, deploying mines — cost
**turns**. Turns reset once every 24 hours. Trading at a port and managing a
planet you own do not cost turns, so you can always find something useful to
do even when you're low.

## The interface

- **Sector** — your current location: warp lanes, the local port, other
  ships, planets, mines, and sector chat.
- **Galaxy Map** — a graphical view of every charted sector and warp lane.
  Tap a sector to see whether it's reachable this turn.
- **Shipyard** — buy a new ship or upgrade fighters, shields, and cargo
  holds. Only available at Stardock (Sector 1).
- **Planet** — manage a planet you've claimed in your current sector.
- **Corporation** — team up with other captains.
- **Leaderboard** — see who's amassed the most credits in the galaxy.

## Staying safe

Federation space (Sector 1) is safe — no combat, and it's where the
Shipyard lives. Everywhere else, you can be attacked by hostile NPCs, rival
captains, or wander into a mine field. If your fighters ever hit zero, your
ship is destroyed and you eject to an escape pod back at Stardock with a
fresh Merchant Cruiser — you keep your credits (minus anything looted) but
lose your cargo.

Good hunting.
