# Corporations

A corporation is a group of captains playing together under a shared name
and tag. Corporations are useful for coordinating trade routes, sharing
sector intel over dedicated corp chat, and eventually pooling resources.

## Founding a corporation

From the **Corporation** tab, any captain not already in one can found a
new corporation with a name (3-64 characters) and a short tag (2-8 letters
or numbers, shown next to your handle on the leaderboard and in sector
listings).

## Joining a corporation

Browse existing corporations from the same tab and join one with a single
tap. You can only belong to one corporation at a time.

## What membership gets you

- A `[TAG]` badge next to your name on the leaderboard.
- Corp-scoped chat, separate from galaxy and sector chat.
- A shared treasury (tracked today; deposit/withdraw tooling is planned).

Leaving a corporation and officer/member role management are on the
roadmap — this release focuses on founding, joining, and shared comms.
