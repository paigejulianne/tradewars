# Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to be online to play?**
The app itself (this manual, the interface, your last-known ship stats)
works offline as an installed web app. Moving, trading, fighting, and chat
all require a connection to the galaxy server, since TradeWars is a shared,
persistent multiplayer world — there's no offline single-player mode.

**Why can't I log in right after registering?**
You must click the verification link sent to your email first. Check spam
if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

**I lost my ship. What now?**
You eject to an escape pod and respawn at Stardock (Sector 1) with a fresh
Merchant Cruiser. You keep your credits (minus anything looted from you)
but lose whatever cargo was aboard.

**Why can't I upgrade my ship or buy a new one?**
Ship purchases and upgrades only happen at Stardock, Sector 1 — fly home
first.

**How do turns work?**
You get 1,000 turns, refreshed once every 24 hours (UTC). Movement costs 1
turn per warp jump (most ships), attacking costs 2, deploying mines costs 1.
Trading and managing a planet you own don't cost turns.

**Is there a way to see the whole galaxy at once?**
Yes — the Galaxy Map tab renders every charted sector and warp lane
graphically. Tap a sector to check if it's reachable, then warp there
directly from the map.

**Where do I report a bug or suggest a feature?**
This is a young, actively developed galaxy — mention it in galaxy chat, or
reach out to the server operator directly.
